# Additional experimental kits — 28 October 2026

These designs precede their implementation. None is part of the default random pool or a claim of a finished release. Umbrakinesis and Kinetic Reservoir were requested as unique powers; both are **mod-original / non-canon**, with no invented official wielder. Blueflame is a main-series power associated with Dabi. Its Japanese label 蒼炎 and blue-flame principle are verified on the [official character profile](https://heroaca.com/character/chara_group06/06-06/); the live page contains identity spoilers omitted from the offline entry. English move labels, heat arithmetic and all progression are adaptations.

## Umbrakinesis

Violet, ink-black and silver; control, distraction and positional defence. Shade is a finite 0–100 budget. It recovers faster in low light and more slowly in bright light; bright-light casting costs 25% more. It is usable in daylight. There is no blindness overlay, wall vision, intangibility or guaranteed escape.

Shadow Lash marks one legitimately hit eligible target for six seconds. Umbral Bind and Shadow Reel require the caster's own visible marked target, consume that mark, and apply a brief resisted slow or a bounded pull. Break sight or step out of range to deny the follow-up. No mark is earned from a dummy, ally, protected NPC or failed damage event. Marks are bounded to four per caster and cleared on interruption of the character's world context.

Dusk Mantle reduces projectile damage for two seconds; melee and environmental hazards bypass it. Silhouette Decoy creates one short-lived, immobile, visibly labelled original shadow mannequin and may distract at most eight nearby hostile mobs with clear sight. It gives no loot, experience or mastery, never controls players, and cannot occupy an entity. Shade Slip and Midnight Stride are complementary grounded footwork, not teleportation. Eclipse Well is a short, telegraphed radial release with cover, control resistance and finite pulses. Every damaging action uses common permission, team, PvP and cover checks.

Eight moves: Shadow Lash, Shade Slip, Umbral Bind, Dusk Mantle, Silhouette Decoy, Shadow Reel, Midnight Stride, Eclipse Well. Standard 0/5/12/20/35/50 field milestones apply. Non-combat use is distraction and protected rescue positioning. The Cadet suit remains equipment. Planned later specialisations: Binding versus Veil, with range versus defence tradeoffs; not yet implemented.

## Kinetic Reservoir

Amber, steel and cream; physical impact storage and counters. Stored impact is 0–60, begins at zero, and decays outside combat. Only a real hostile physical or projectile impact caught during a live guard stores a capped part of the prevented damage. Self, allied, environmental and scripted harmless damage cannot charge it. No reflected damage recursion or automatic full immunity exists. Guard fatigue prevents continuous absorbing; ordinary weapons and an uncharged physical jab remain useful.

Absorb Brace, Damping Guard and Kinetic Bulwark provide finite 20%, 25% and 40% reduction windows against eligible impacts. At most ten stored energy is earned from one hit, up to sixty total. Hits add guard fatigue; reaching 100 breaks the guard. Counter Release, Stored Vault, Impact Wave and Singular Release spend earned impact at cast reservation. Missing or interrupted attacks do not refund it. Stored Vault applies a bounded grounded jump impulse, subject to normal server collision; it is not flight. Recoil Step and Inertial Jab are complementary physical training.

Nine moves: Absorb Brace, Inertial Jab, Recoil Step, Counter Release, Damping Guard, Stored Vault, Impact Wave, Kinetic Bulwark, Singular Release. First useful offensive move is a starter. Common field milestones apply, and defence is available to solo support builds. Distinct weakness: empty storage limits ranged output; cover and delayed attacks punish guard timing. Planned later specialisations: Counter versus Rescuer; not yet implemented.

## Blueflame

Cobalt, cyan and pale blue; ranged pressure with deliberate thermal recovery. Heat begins at zero, rises from casting and prevents another cast if its cost would exceed 100. Controlled Cooling is an exposed two-second recovery technique. This finite recovery is an explicit gameplay adaptation of a dangerous power; no lore claim about immunity is made. No terrain fires or block destruction. Underwater fire is refused. Grounded footwork remains usable underwater and without heat.

Eight moves: Cobalt Stream, Ash Step, Blue Lance, Cinder Fan, Controlled Cooling, Azure Ring, Scorched Retreat, Bluefire Front. Stream and capstone have finite server pulse windows; ordinary hurt resistance still applies, so the listed damage is per successful pulse rather than guaranteed total. Cover, range and startup/recovery remain counters. Basic user and source information appears offline without late-series identity/form spoilers. No TV first-demonstration episode is invented.
