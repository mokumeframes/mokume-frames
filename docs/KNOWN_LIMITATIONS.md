# Current boundaries — do not advertise a finished mod

This snapshot implements a verified build foundation and part of the first playable loop. **Neither Milestone 0's full visual integration gate nor Milestone 1's full acceptance gate is complete.** The complete requested project remains in MASTER_BRIEF.txt and the content/region matrices.

- Only Explosion has gameplay implementation. All eight moves now have server-tested starter/trial acquisition; the final visual integration gate is still pending. Movie, original and all other main-series quirks are not registered or placed in rolls.
- The school route currently ends after enrolment, control/support training, a short traversal course and first aid, followed by three optional quirk trials. It does not implement entrance robots, USJ, Sports Festival, internships, licence examinations, graduation, villain/independent campaigns or a boss.
- The authored starter is 144×104 blocks with two classroom wings, central connector, workshop, infirmary, dorm module, training lanes, a ground course and a city block. It is not the complete U.A. campus or the requested regional/movie world. Exact canon architecture/geography is not claimed.
- Minecraft's GameTestServer deliberately discards data-pack dimensions when making its flat test world. The map builder therefore runs through an explicit GameTest-only hook in that disposable world. The real 1.21.1 dimension codec is separately tested. Normal client/server travel has not been manually exercised.
- The base client reached the title/world-creation screens with the exact library stack **before** later gameplay/menu changes. The final custom menus, loading mixins, Photon emitter and armour transfer are compiled but have not been manually observed in-game. Desktop control stopped when requested and was not resumed.
- HTML/PNG interface previews are design references, clearly labelled as such. They do not prove that Minecraft renders the final UI correctly. Real GUI-scale, clipping, narration and input checks remain pending.
- The compact move/stamina/strain HUD preserves vanilla survival displays. Its editor now includes draggable anchors, Alt+arrow positioning, scale, opacity, compact mode, numeric resources, an objective toggle and reset/save/cancel. The full custom hotbar, independent quest-panel positioning, broader layout presets and complete accessibility certification remain unfinished.
- No authenticated two-client observation, standard/slim rig matrix, first-person/remote playback, swimming/riding/held-item integration or interrupted-animation visual pass has been completed. The suit's explicit segmented bend transfer needs this validation.
- Source geometry, animations, icons, menu artwork and six original synthesised sounds with WAV masters/provenance are present. A validated Photon editor project/`.fx` export, final audio mix, official-style character costumes and complete asset production pipeline are unfinished.
- Guidebook gameplay numbers are synced from effective server data. The Explosion source notebook, bookmarks and basic source-season spoiler filtering exist. Broader source/user/media filters, full illustrations and complete lore/localisation verification remain unfinished. No first-demonstration episode has been invented.
- Reroll offers/cost/archive logic is tested with synthetic alternative IDs. There is no real alternative quirk in this build, so ordinary rerolls refuse without charge. Further earn routes, targeted attunement and the 45–90 minute economy target are not play-tested. The 250-credit exchange is reachable from the reroll page at the registrar, but refuses without spending credits while no alternative quirk is enabled.
- Loadout edits outside combat/recovery, ability cooldowns and three durable presets per quirk exist. Direct/select-and-cast parity, duels, geographic safe-area policy, custom guard/dodge framework and broader equipment progression are pending.
- First aid is a timed proximity interaction, not a full rescue simulation. Enrolment currently selects the hero path; meaningful allegiance changes and comparable independent/villain access are future content.
- Player schema v2 includes an idempotent, tested v1 migration that preserves durable progression, balances, offers and cooldowns. Unknown future versions are rejected. No broader legacy-format conversion is claimed.
- No eight-user performance benchmark, frame-time capture, sustained memory test, anti-cheat integration, third-party protection bridge or release multiplayer certification has been performed. Accelerated GameTest completion times are not a performance benchmark.

Do not expand the advertised completed roster to get around these gates. Next: validate the final presentation and real network play, finish the M1 acquisition/guide/asset criteria, then implement M2's three additional quirks and school/villain chains in order.
