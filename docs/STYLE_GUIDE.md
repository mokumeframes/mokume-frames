# Visual direction

Interface: ink navy #111B27, paper #F2EDE2, muted steel #8294A5, hero teal #55D3BC, Explosion orange #FF974F. Danger uses a striped border plus a label, not colour alone. Use Minecraft's default font at native GUI scale; never rasterise text or key names. Use an 8-unit spacing grid and crisp 24-unit technique silhouettes.

Combat: preserve the central aiming area. Orange spark wedges describe Explosion direction; a thin amber ring describes charge. Damage areas are server-defined and should remain legible when decorative particles are reduced. No compulsory camera shake or screen flash. Short, quiet tells precede heavier hits; don't mask subtitles or speech.

Equipment: original navy training suit, teal chest seam, cream hard plates, orange blast gauntlets. Rigid plates cover upper/lower limbs separately with an exposed joint. Sources remain editable and the runtime rig uses GeckoLib 4 armour bone names. This is an original academy training costume, not an official character costume.

Scale: technique panel should remain compact beside the vanilla survival HUD until replacement hotbar parity is verified. Do not hide air, armour, absorption, mount or spectator information for a visual prototype. Full custom hotbar replacement is a later acceptance gate.

Audio: original synthesised short burst and charge cues, no official voices/music. Suggested default mix: tell -16 dBFS peak, impact -10 dBFS peak; use player sound category with distance attenuation. Verify in-game before claiming audio balance.
